Import student roster
For new students: email, name, class roll, batch (e.g. 2022-26), subjects enrolled (comma separated codes, e.g. DSA, DBMS, AI) and guardian mobile — the WhatsApp number that receives low-attendance alerts. exam roll, mobile number, guardian name and guardian email are optional. Header spelling is flexible; extra columns are ignored.
Updating students in bulk. A row whose email already exists updates that student rather than creating a second one — so you can upload a sheet of just email + guardian mobile and only guardian mobiles change. A blank cell leaves the current value alone; put a single - to clear it. Leaving subjects enrolled blank keeps their existing enrolments.
Add your subjects and batches before importing — unknown subject codes and unknown batches are both reported as errors rather than created, so a typo cannot quietly split a class in two. Only new students are emailed an activation link; updating an existing one sends nothing. Download the template.
